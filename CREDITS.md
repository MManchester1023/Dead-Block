## Mixamo (`assets-src/models/mixamo/`)

Rigs and animations from [Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com) (Adobe), downloaded by the project owner. **In the game:** riflemen and engineers (since round 10: Kolos Studios' and MadTrollStudio's soldiers, rigged on Mixamo) with Rifle Idle, Walking, Firing Rifle, Dying and Working On Device; soldiers also use Shoot Rifle (walking while firing), Taking Cover, Taking Cover Idle and Idle Crouching Aiming, and since v4.55 Fire Rifle (kneeling), Crouch To Standing With Rifle, Death Crouching Headshot Front, Freehang Climb and Climbing Down (since v4.63 Jumping Down instead, to leave a tower; since v4.62 Rifle Crouch Walk, replacing Walk Crouching Forward Right); civilians (since v4.62 the Casual LowPoly models, rigged on Mixamo) use Breathing Idle and Slow Run; medics (v4.62) use Pistol Idle, Pistol Run, Pistol Kneeling Idle, Pistol Kneel To Stand and a medic healing animation; female zombies use Zombie Idle, Walking, Zombie Run, Zombie Attack and Zombie Dying (Zombie Punching kept in `assets-src/models/` as an alternative). Mixamo's terms allow royalty-free use in games; the raw files may not be redistributed as a standalone asset pack.

## Trees and shrubs (v4.66)

"Low Poly Forest Tree Pack" by **99.Miles**, uploaded by the owner (`assets-src/models/low-poly-forest-tree-pack.zip`; licence to fill in). **Changed:** the FBX unpacked from its .rar (`assets-src/models/forest_tree_pack/Tree_Pack.fbx`); 13 trees (four with separate trunk and branches, nine single-piece trees from the pack's atlas) and 3 bushes as one GLB each, each atlas tree with only its own crop of the texture, opacity folded into the alpha, textures ≤ 256 px, trees scaled to 6–9.5 m and bushes to 1–1.6 m (`src/dev/treeExport.ts` exportTexturedTrees, `tools/assets/export-forest.cjs`). The rocks aren't used. Every tree in the game is one of these since v4.66. Game copies in `public/models/trees/`.

**No longer used (v4.58–v4.65):** two low-poly tree packs uploaded by the owner (`assets-src/models/low-poly-tree-pack.zip`, `low-poly-tree-set.zip`), **free to use** per the owner (authors to fill in). **Changed:** each tree merged with its material colours baked in, big ones simplified to ~900 triangles, trees scaled to 6–9.5 m and three small ones used as shrubs at 1–1.6 m; from v4.59 to v4.65 every tree in the game was one of these (`tools/assets/export-trees.cjs`); replaced by the forest pack at the owner's request.
