## Districts and their descriptions

- Roadside Motel:

Large motel surrounded by residential. Abandonded and crashed cars are littered about. Not many zombies in this area as its on the edge of town.

- Gas Station Strip:

Some commercial, convenience stores, and the gas station next door to a small hospital.

- Rail Siding:

Box cars and a depot at train depot just outside the city. They used this place heavily to get supplies in and out of the city when the virus first broke out. There still a ton of crates and junk littered about. This district is also home to a power station that was one of the last to go before everyone pulled out.

- Industrial Park:

Warehouses and a railyard with tracks connecting to Rail Siding. Knocked over train cars, broken crates, industrial equiptment, supplies, all over the place. This was the main supply hub for the city when shit hit the fan. All hell broke loose between the looting and chaos in the industrial park. The warehouses are towering brick buildings with large smokestacks and junk and equiptment everywhere.

- Trailer Park:

Tight rows and little cover. This place was hell on earth even before the outbreak. Housing most of the workers at the industrial park.

- Suburbs:

Where the city starts to get a bit nicer. This residential district housed most of those who lived in the city. This place was kept a bit nicer than the rest but is still pretty trashed. Cars everywhere crashed and abandonded in the street and peoples driveways.

- Market:

Small shops, parking lots, food and drink stores. The main  commercial hub of the city.

- Parking Garage:

Parking garage for the city and the market. Plenty of abandonded cars to search and dark tight corners to be afraid of.

- Main Street:

Narrow Shops and alleys

- Checkpoint:

Fortified highway interchange

- Hospital Row:

Large hospital with parking garage across the street

- Stadium:

Large football stadium and parking lots

- Harbor:

Docks and shipping containers

- Airport:

Runways, hangers, and admin buildings.

- Downtown:

Skyscrapers and downtown commercial. Final district; Patient Zero largest of all districts and dangerous enough that players will avoid it until the having the airfield.
